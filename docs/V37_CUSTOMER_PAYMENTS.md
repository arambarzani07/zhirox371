# V37 — paginated customer payments and truthful cash classification

The customer-payment card now reads payment_received ledger rows directly through the shared verified history helper, independently from the last 500 cached ledger entries. Pages contain 50 rows with exact RLS-scoped counts and stable created_at/id order. Customer, exact phone, Baghdad date and cash_drawer/ledger_only method filters execute before pagination. Text search explicitly covers the current page only. Inactive customers and customers outside the loaded mutation directory are identified through embedded customer metadata.

Metrics now describe visible page rows: payment face amounts, drawer-cash amounts, payment count and ledger-only amounts. Ledger-only discounts are not presented as cash receipts. Null/unrecognized legacy methods appear as unknown with a separate visible count/amount; they are not assigned to drawer cash or ledger-only totals. Payment totals use checked integers and BigInt accumulation and reject positive debt deltas or non-payment rows.

Vouchers come only from the current verified payment page. They show the full ledger entry identity and the authoritative balance_after_minor of that entry, not a current customer balance or cached fallback. Ledger-only vouchers are titled as account reductions; unknown-method vouchers make no cash claim. Page/filter refresh, loading, read failure and logout clear printable content and rows. Request/store/actor guards ignore obsolete responses. An emptied final page moves to the last remaining page. Offset pagination with a fixed upper timestamp is not a repeatable-read snapshot across concurrent backdated inserts/deletes.

CSV uses the same visible rows and includes signed debt change, per-entry historical balance, explicit method label, full entry identity and Baghdad timestamps. Filenames identify the source page range and matching payment count. Successful financial writes refresh the verified page before optionally displaying a matching voucher. Existing financial RPCs and durable request/retry logic are unchanged. Opening payment from a directory/debt row now waits for fresh customer selectors before selecting the requested customer and amount, preventing an async refresh from restoring a previous payer selection.

No production database, schema, grants, RLS policies, hardware or locked Home assets are changed. The first 500 active customers still bound the mutation selectors/directory; full historical account statements remain unfinished. Unknown-method classification is based on stored data and cannot reconstruct legacy missing payment metadata.

Local checks cover 623 payment rows across 13 pages, type/method/phone scope, archived customer identity, separated cash/ledger/unknown totals, unsafe money, stale read/logout handling, final-page recovery, verified voucher lookup and fresh form selection. The real bundled PC preview was checked with 523 payments through the final 23 rows, historical ledger-only vouchers, cash/phone filters, empty states and downloaded CSV. New headless-browser and disposable local Auth/JWT/PostgREST checks are included for CI; remote CI has not run on the local changes.
